BTH Journal Publication

BRIHASPATI: An AI-Powered Cyber Forensic Web Application for Detecting Illegal Content on Mobile Devices

Journal: Bulletin for Technology and History Date: 05 May 2025 Author: Narendra Chaudhary

Abstract

Mobile digital forensics investigations are frequently bottlenecked by the sheer volume of multimedia data on modern Android smartphones. This research proposes and implements BRIHASPATI, a full-stack automated cyber forensic web application. The platform provides non-invasive physical and logical data acquisition via Android Debug Bridge (ADB), integrates the Autopsy digital forensic framework for parsing file systems, and runs a custom Computer Vision deep learning classifier to automatically scan and flag suspicious or illegal multimedia files. Experimental results showed investigative audit speed was boosted significantly, reducing investigator manual review burden while enhancing precision.

System Architecture Pillars

Automated ADB Data Pull Pipeline
Autopsy Evidence Indexing
AI Image & Video Classification
